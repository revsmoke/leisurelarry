# Original protagonist objective

Research date: 2026-09-22. This note answers what the remake's objective should be if it follows the original games. It does not change the game.

## Primary evidence

- **Softporn Adventure, original Apple II release:** Blue Sky Software's original brochure/manual, PDF page 2, introduces an unnamed vacationer in Lost Vagueness whose ambition is to seduce three women before returning home. Page 4 repeats the objective: “Find and seduce 3 girls!!” Its supporting challenges are earning money, finding the women, avoiding dangers, and solving puzzles. This is the original publisher's printed material, preserved by the Museum of Computer Adventure Game History, rather than a later walkthrough. [Original Blue Sky Software manual, PDF pages 2 and 4](https://www.mocagh.org/sierra/softporn-alt.pdf).
- **Leisure Suit Larry in the Land of the Lounge Lizards:** Sierra's original manual, PDF page 4, explicitly makes Larry's main goal to “find and seduce the girl of your dreams.” It gives him one night and identifies money, opportunity, and bad breath as obstacles. This establishes sexual and romantic pursuit as the central motivation, rather than merely making friends or reaching a location. [Original Sierra manual, PDF page 4](https://www.mocagh.org/sierra/lsl-manual.pdf).
- **Later corroboration, not original Apple II evidence:** Gary Thompson's DOS version 2.4 manual describes the goal as to “score” three times. This agrees with the original premise, but its author identifies it as a rewritten and redesigned port, so it should not substitute for the original manual when claiming exact Apple II behavior. [Gary Thompson DOS manual](https://www.abandonwaredos.com/docawd.php?idg=1123&sf=softporn_manual.txt&sg=Softporn+Adventure&st=manual).

## Design conclusion

To retain the originals' central premise, Larry should start the night **trying to get laid, and ultimately seduce the woman of his dreams**. The supporting adventure is a series of flirtations, setbacks, humiliations, misunderstandings, and inventory puzzles that help or frustrate that ambition. Softporn's three-woman premise and Larry's dream-woman objective are related, but are not identical completion specifications; the remake need not treat a three-encounter quota as a canonical Larry requirement.

A contemporary implementation can preserve this overtly adult motivation while making mutual attraction and consent part of the interactions. Larry's vanity, bad assumptions, and ridiculous persistence are strong targets for comedy. Connection or personal growth can enrich the outcome, but substituting them for the sexual premise would be a deliberate reinterpretation of the originals. These are design recommendations inferred from the cited objectives, not claims that a particular mechanic is in either original.

The manual establishes the stated objective, not every exact winning condition in the executable. Claims about the final Eve sequence or precisely when Larry loses his virginity require game-text or playthrough verification beyond this focused note. No new executable playthrough was performed for this research.
