# Last Call in Lost Wages — complete walkthrough

This is an original, non-explicit adaptation of the puzzle-adventure structure of *Softporn Adventure* and *Leisure Suit Larry in the Land of the Lounge Lizards*. There is no death timer or inventory limit, and the cashier can help you recover from bankruptcy.

Choose **Look**, **Talk**, **Take**, or **Use**, then click a scene target. Select a carried item and click another target to combine them. Right-click an inventory item to examine it; double-click the apple core to extract its seeds. Use **Take** for loose objects; use **Talk** for people and **Use** for machines or activities. To purchase whiskey, **Use Lefty** or type **BUY WHISKEY**. The parser also accepts these actions.

Named exits connect rooms; the Neon Strip is the hub, and **City Map** follows unlocked routes. **Need a nudge?** offers increasingly specific hints; the third level gives an explicit solution with spoilers. **Notebook** preserves discovered clues and completed favors. **Save** and **Load** use a manual slot; when an autosave exists, **Continue evening** appears on the next launch. **? → Restore autosave** also restores that separate slot. Scroll within Help to read all its instructions.

## Before the night begins

Choose Larry or Lisa and your orientation (default bisexual). The rooftop host is Eve or Adam; use the displayed name in dialogue and commands. Heterosexual and bisexual Larry end with Eve; homosexual Larry ends with Adam. Heterosexual and bisexual Lisa end with Adam; homosexual Lisa ends with Eve. Below, **Eve** means your selected rooftop host.

The goal is to get laid with that host. Three optional flings are available, but none substitutes for the rooftop finale. Friendship and postponing the final invitation keep the night open.

## 1. A suit, a paper, and a bar

1. On the Strip, **take newspaper**, then **read newspaper**. Learn how the hotel's experimental instant-fruit planter works.
2. **Buy flowers** for $10.
3. **Go bar**. Choose **Use** and click **Lefty**, or type **buy whiskey**, to spend $10. **Give whiskey to regular** to receive the TV remote. **Take Lefty** does not make a purchase.
4. **Go bathroom**. **Read graffiti** to learn **BELLYBUTTON**. **Take ring** from the free costume-jewelry dish.
5. **Go bar**. Type **BELLYBUTTON** or talk to the bouncer. **Use remote on TV** to tune in championship bowling.
6. **Go backroom** and **take candy**. The fire escape needs a safety rope; you will return.
7. **Go bar**, then **go street**.

## 2. The casino and Didi's opening night

8. **Go casino** and **take pass**. This complimentary pass admits you to Studio 69.
9. For the full score, type **play slots** and spin at least once. Each spin costs $5. The repeating payout cycle is $0, $15, $0, $25, so four spins add $20 to your balance.
10. If your wallet falls below $10, **talk cashier** for a $20 recovery grant. It can be claimed whenever funds fall below the threshold. The entire required shopping list costs $32, against an initial wallet of $80.
11. **Go street**, then **go disco**. **Talk to Didi**, then **dance**.
12. **Give ring to Didi**, **give candy to Didi**, and **give flowers to Didi**. She needs these as cabaret props.
13. Didi shares the stage manager's number. At the disco, **call 555-0987**. The stage manager points you toward the spare rope. You still need a cutting tool. Optionally **talk Didi** again to pass along his dance invitation and hear how the opening is coming together; this adds no required errand or points.

## 3. Wine, stagecraft, and decent coffee

14. **Go street**, then **go shop**. **Buy wine** for $12.
15. **Go street**, then **go alley**. **Give wine to busker**. He trades you a pocket knife.
16. **Take rubber mallet** from the maintenance rack. **Take apple core**, then **use apple core** to separate its seeds.
17. **Go street**, then **go disco**. **Use knife on spare rope** to cut the packaging cord and take the coil.
18. **Go street**, **go bar**, **go backroom**. **Use rope on railing** to secure a safety line.
19. **Go balcony**. **Use mallet on window** to loosen it. **Take voucher** from the open service window.
20. **Go backroom**, **go bar**, **go street**, **go shop**. **Use voucher on espresso machine** to receive an espresso.
21. **Go street**, **go hotel**. **Give coffee to receptionist**. After a welcome break, she invites you to the rooftop gathering and grants penthouse access.

## 4. An apple and a sunrise

22. From the hotel, **go penthouse**, then **go rooftop**. **Talk Eve** to introduce yourself. She missed dinner and asks for something fresh; this gives your gardening project a purpose.
23. **Go penthouse**, **go hotel**, **go garden**. **Take stool**. **Plant seeds** in the experimental planter; the newspaper supplies its instructions.
24. **Go hotel**, then **go penthouse**. **Use stool on cabinet**. **Take pitcher**, then **fill pitcher** at the sink.
25. **Go hotel**, **go garden**. **Water planter**. The experimental machine grows a miniature apple tree. **Take apple**; growing it does not automatically put the fruit in your pockets.
26. **Go hotel**, **go penthouse**, **go rooftop**. **Give apple to Eve**.
27. **Talk Eve** to tell her about your dance with Didi.
28. **Talk Eve** again to listen to her story about the hotel's gardens and experimental planter.
29. **Talk Eve** a third time after giving the apple or choose **Ask Eve/Adam to get laid: a private sunrise for two** to accept the final invitation. Watch or skip the private-scene cutscene; the outcome completes the game.

The route above earns **100 / 100 points** across 25 unique milestones. The three final conversations advance one exchange; only its conclusion awards the ending points. Repeating completed actions cannot duplicate rewards. Earlier gardening is also valid—the rooftop introduction is guidance, not a new gate. Taking every scenic route is optional; the ending waits for you.

## Optional encounters

For any selected profile, each optional NPC's displayed name and gender are compatible with the chosen orientation. Bisexual characters meet both genders across these detours.

| Location | Clue to examine | Solution offered in conversation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Backstage Lounge | Dressing-screen tag | Turn the rhinestone latch |
| Lucky Chip Casino | Lucky napkin | Suggest the hearts card |
| Moonlight Garden | Lantern instruction card | Set the portrait lantern to amber |

TALK to the person, LOOK at the clue, and choose the solution in conversation. A wrong answer preserves your progress. Choose the flirtation to receive an invitation, then **Yes — let’s get laid**, or take a rain check. Declining has no penalty and a later flirtation reopens the choice. Accepted encounters have a skippable scene and durable memory; they grant no score and do not finish the evening. Afterward, you can share an in-joke rather than replaying the encounter.

## Optional casino games

In the casino, choose **Use** and click the slots or blackjack table, or type **play slots** / **play blackjack** to open the game panel.

- **Slots:** animated reels, $5 per spin, and a fixed four-spin payout cycle of $0, $15, $0, $25. One spin earns the optional four-point milestone.
- **Blackjack:** choose a $5, $10 or $20 stake, then Deal, Hit or Stand. Aces count as 1 or 11; the dealer stands on 17. A natural blackjack pays 3:2 profit rounded down to whole dollars ($7 on a $5 stake); a normal win pays even money and a push returns the stake. The deck is shuffled for each hand. There are no split, double-down or insurance actions.

Closing a blackjack hand before settlement returns its stake. Blackjack is optional and does not add puzzle points. Both games use the adventure wallet; talk to the cashier below $10 for the $20 recovery grant.

## Running the automated walkthrough

From the project root, use Godot 4:

```sh
godot --headless --path . --script tests/test_game.gd
```

On macOS with the bundled application:

```sh
/Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot --headless --path . --script tests/test_game.gd
```

The test covers the entire route, room gates, every scene target's examination response, item trades, repeated rewards, bankruptcy recovery, save/load, reset, and malformed saves.
